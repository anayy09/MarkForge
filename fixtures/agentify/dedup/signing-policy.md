# Electronic Signing Policy

## Scope

This policy governs every document the sealing service issues for electronic signature.

## Validity

A signing request must lapse 30 days from issue.

A reminder must be dispatched 21 days ahead of expiry.

A sealed document must never be re-issued under the same reference.

## Records

The audit trail for a sealed document must be preserved for 90 days.

## Withdrawal

A revocation must take effect within 3 hours of the instruction.
