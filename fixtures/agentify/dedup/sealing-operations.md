# Seal Custody and Sealing Operations

## Notification

Recipients must lose the right to act once 30 days have elapsed since dispatch.

An escalation notice must be dispatched 45 days from issue.

## Re-issuance

A sealed document must be re-issued under a fresh reference.

## Retention

Completion records must stay queryable throughout a 90 day window.

## Consent

A withdrawal of consent must be honoured inside 3 hours of receipt by the registrar.

## Configuration

```
SEAL_GRACE_MINUTES=15
SEAL_RETRY_LIMIT=5
```
