# Facilities Inspection Report

## Summary of Findings

The inspection of the north building took place over two days and covered the loading dock, the plant room, and both stairwells. Access to the roof was not possible because the hatch remains sealed, so the roof survey is outstanding and should be scheduled before the end of the quarter.

Three items require attention within thirty days. None of them presents an immediate hazard, but the drainage issue in the plant room will worsen with the autumn rainfall and is cheaper to correct now than after the floor slab has been saturated for a season.

## Items Requiring Attention

- The floor drain in the plant room is partly blocked and standing water was present at both visits.
- Two emergency lights in the east stairwell did not illuminate during the discharge test.
- The dock levelling plate is worn at the hinge and should be replaced rather than repaired.

## Items Cleared

- The fire door on the second landing now closes fully and latches without assistance.
- Guarding on the compressor was refitted after the previous inspection and is correct.

## Method

Each item was inspected visually and, where practical, tested under load. The emergency lighting was checked by isolating the local supply and timing the discharge. Measurements were taken with a calibrated meter and the calibration certificate is held with the site file.

## Next Steps

Arrange roof access through the building manager. Obtain two quotations for the levelling plate before committing to a supplier, since the wear pattern suggests the original part was under specified for the traffic it carries.
