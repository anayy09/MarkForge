# Unicode Edge Cases

Combining marks: café, résumé, naïve.

Emoji including ZWJ sequences and skin tones: 👋🏽 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 🇬🇧

Non-breaking spaces are semantic: 10 km, 5 kg, Mr. Smith.

CJK text: 日本語のテキストと中文文本。

Right-to-left: مرحبا بالعالم and עברית.

Mixed direction: The word مرحبا appears inside English text.
