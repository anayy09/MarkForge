# List Structures

Ordinary ordered list:

1. First item
2. Second item
3. Third item

Nested to three levels:

- Top level
  - Second level
    - Third level
  - Back to second
- Top level again

Mixed nesting:

1. Ordered parent
   - Unordered child
   - Another child
2. Second ordered parent

A list interrupted by a paragraph:

1. Before the interruption

This paragraph interrupts the list.

1. After the interruption

Ordered list starting at seven:

7. Seventh
8. Eighth
