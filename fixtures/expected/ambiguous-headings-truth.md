# Site Assessment

This paragraph is ordinary body text at the document default size.

#### Scope

Three sites were assessed against the current standard.

**Note that the following applies to all three sites**

Access arrangements were unchanged from the previous assessment.

#### Method

Each site was walked with the standard checklist.

Readings were taken at the start and end of each visit.

No site required a follow-up visit within the assessment window.

#### What was measured

Temperature, humidity, and airflow at three fixed points per site.
